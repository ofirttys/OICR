OTC Care Pathway: Reach Analysis

Tracking patient flow through the Ovarian Tissue Cryopreservation pathway
Study Period: May 2025 - May 2027
Oncology Team
Paediatric Gynaecology
REI at MSF
Collaborative (MSF + Ped Gyn)
1
Diagnosis & Eligibility
Patients with ovaries newly diagnosed with cancer or requiring gonadotoxic treatment for benign indication
2
Fertility Risk Assessment
Patient receives formal fertility risk assessment based on diagnosis and planned treatment
3
Initial Fertility Discussion
Primary oncologist discusses fertility preservation options and risks with patient/family
4
Referral to Paediatric Gynaecology
Patient referred to paediatric gynaecology for specialized fertility preservation consultation
5
Paediatric Gynaecology Consultation
Initial consultation with paediatric gynaecology team to discuss OTC procedure and options
6
Referral to Mount Sinai Fertility (MSF)
Patient referred to MSF for comprehensive fertility preservation assessment and counseling
7
MSF Consultation
Detailed consultation at Mount Sinai Fertility clinic regarding fertility preservation options and long-term planning
8
Referral Back to Paediatric Gynaecology
Patient referred back to paediatric gynaecology to initiate OTC procedure scheduling
9
Surgery Booking & Pre-Procedural Care
Surgery scheduled, patient receives necessary pre-operative assessments and preparation from paediatric gynaecology team
10
OTC Procedure & Tissue Processing
Patient undergoes ovarian tissue cryopreservation surgical procedure (tissue harvesting by paediatric gynaecology), followed by tissue processing and cryogenic storage at MSF
Reach Metric Definition
Reach measures the number and proportion of eligible patients who complete each step of the OTC care pathway. For each step, we calculate: (1) the absolute number of patients reaching that step, and (2) the proportion relative to the initial eligible population. This allows us to identify potential attrition points and optimize the pathway for maximum patient access to fertility preservation services.
